# An ADR holds one decision

*2026-10-11, restating a decision of 2026-09-27*

## Context

An ADR that held several decisions could not be replaced when one of them
changed: what still held would go with it.

## Decision

An ADR holds one decision.

## Consequences

Replacing a decision can mean writing several ADRs at once, one for each
decision of the old ADR that still holds.
