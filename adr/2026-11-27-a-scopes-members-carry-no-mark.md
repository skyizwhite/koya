# A scope's members carry no mark

*2026-11-27, restating a decision of 2026-11-27*

## Context

Nomini sent every member of a scope with a name of letters with a request, so
koya marked the members a request must not carry with a leading underscore.
Nagi sends only what a request is given.

## Decision

A scope's members are named for what they are, with no mark of their own.

## Consequences

- A member's name says what it holds, not whether a request carries it.
- What a request sends is written where it is sent.
